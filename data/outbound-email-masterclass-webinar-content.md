200, 300, 400k emails a month.

Google flags two things at that volume, and most teams only know about one.

The first is sameness. When every email looks identical to the next, Google notices. The fix is to build small pieces of variability into each send: first name, company name, sender name, and spintax snippets.

The second is a subject line that disconnects from the body. If the subject talks about one thing and the message says another, Google can flag it as spam.

Variability in the body, consistency between subject and message. That's the balance for broad campaigns.

### Unify University — Two Google spam flags in high-volume cold email
- At 200-400k emails a month, Google flags a lack of variability from one email to the next.
