```
Perfect outreach doesn't exist.

When we launch an AI outreach agent, I delay the sequence first. You can do this natively in Unify.

Then I read the emails and check the accuracy. I'm making sure it's not doing anything wacky.

If a reply comes in saying "we didn't launch this product," or the reference is from X months ago, I tweak the agent and keep testing.

And yes, there will be edge cases. We'll probably get one every few thousand.

It's well worth it, based on the success.

Zooming out, it's not going to be 100% perfect. But if it's 95% to 98% accurate, that makes complete sense, especially in terms of ROI.

Most teams stall because they're chasing a standard that manual outreach never met either.

The real question isn't whether the agent makes mistakes. It's whether you catch the pattern, fix the agent, and keep shipping.

Delay. Audit. Tweak. Repeat.
```

### Carousel
- Slide 1: 100% accurate AI outreach is a myth worth dropping
- Slide 2: Launch the sequence delayed, never straight to send
- Slide 3: Read the emails and check for anything wacky
- Slide 4: A reply says wrong product? Tweak the agent, keep testing
- Slide 5: Edge cases happen. Probably one every few thousand
- Slide 6: 95% to 98% accurate makes complete sense for ROI
- Slide 7: Delay. Audit. Tweak. Repeat. Then let it run

### Unify University — Ship AI outreach at 95%, not never at 100%
- Delay the sequence at launch so you can check every email's accuracy before anything sends.
- Treat bad replies as feedback: tweak the agent and keep testing instead of scrapping it.
- Edge cases are the cost of doing business. At 95% to 98% accuracy, the ROI still makes complete sense.

**Exercise:** Set up your next AI outreach sequence with a send delay. Review the drafted emails, list every error type you spot, and update your agent's instructions to fix the top one before launch.
