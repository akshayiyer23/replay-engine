# Unify — Company Context (for unify-moment-miner)

Use this file to judge whether a webinar moment fits Unify's story, and to write
content angles that quietly connect back to what Unify sells. Never turn a moment
into a product pitch.

---

## 1. What Unify is (one line)
Unify is "Claude for outbound sellers": one chat where an AE or SDR can find the
right accounts, research them, get contact info, and send personalized outreach.

- Founded Jan 2023 by Austin Hughes (CEO, ex-Ramp growth) and Connor Heggie (CTO, ex-Scale AI)
- HQ San Francisco, 51–200 employees
- Raised $40M Series B (Battery Ventures; also OpenAI Startup Fund, Thrive, Emergence)
- Mission: "help the best products win" / "turn growth into a science"
- North star: positive interactions with prospects (not opens, not raw conversion)

## 2. The big messages (what every piece of content should ladder up to)
1. **Every rep can be a GTM engineer.** Sellers no longer need RevOps to build lists, enrich, and sequence.
2. **Warm outbound beats cold outbound.** Reach out with a real reason (a signal), at the right time.
3. **Escape tab hell.** One platform instead of 5+ stitched-together tools.
4. **AI does the busywork, humans do the selling.** Sales is a people job; AI is the toolkit.
5. **Signals > generic intent.** Custom, specific signals win over generic "intent data."

## 9. Voice and tone
- Direct, practical, a little playful (poker nights, "Hit or Miss" happy hours, ASMR demo video)
- Teaches, doesn't sell — Austin's posts pitch a better way to build pipeline, not Unify
- Specific over vague: real numbers, real tools, real steps
- Human-first: Austin has said he writes his own posts without AI — drafts must sound like a person, not a brochure

## 10. Competitors (never praise, never name in a way that promotes them)
Apollo, Clay, Amplemarket, Gong Engage, Instantly, Nooks, Outreach, Salesloft,
ZoomInfo, 6sense. Mentioning a category ("legacy sequencers," "5 stitched tools")
is fine.

## 11. Hard rules for content angles
- No feature lists or "Unify can do X" pitches inside the post
- No private customer details beyond the public proof points above
- The idea must stand alone for someone who never heard of Unify
- Each angle names: pillar, persona, and the product it quietly connects to
