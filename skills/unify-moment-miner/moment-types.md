# The 6 Moment Types

Use these to label candidates. Examples are illustrative patterns, not quotes.

## 1. contrarian
Goes against what most people in GTM believe.
- Signal words: "most people think," "everyone says... but," "unpopular opinion," "stop doing"
- Pattern: "Generic intent data doesn't tell you who's buying. It tells you who's browsing."
- Why it works: it creates tension. People comment to agree or argue.

## 2. stat
A specific number that surprises or proves a point.
- Signal words: a %, $, "x times," a count, a before/after
- Pattern: "Product usage signals got a 9% positive reply rate, the highest of anything we tested."
- Why it works: specific numbers feel credible and are easy to repeat.
- Must include the context for the number in the clip.

## 3. how_to
A concrete, repeatable process the viewer can copy today.
- Signal words: "here's exactly how," "step one," "the way we do it," "the prompt we use"
- Pattern: "Here's how we build a custom signal: pick the trigger, define what 'good' looks like, then..."
- Why it works: people save posts they want to use later.
- Must contain at least 2 real steps, not just "you should do X."

## 4. story
A short real example with a beginning, a turn, and a result.
- Signal words: "we had a customer," "when I was at," "last quarter we," "I remember"
- Pattern: "We ran this play for a customer, and in two weeks it booked more meetings than their whole SDR team."
- Why it works: stories hold attention to the end.
- Must have a result. A story with no ending is not a candidate.

## 5. mistake
A failure or lesson learned the hard way.
- Signal words: "the biggest mistake," "we got this wrong," "don't do what we did," "what I'd change"
- Pattern: "Our first sequences were all automated, and reply rates tanked. Here's what we changed."
- Why it works: honesty builds trust, and people want to avoid the same mistake.

## 6. prediction
A clear view on where GTM is heading.
- Signal words: "in a year," "the future of," "this is going away," "every team will"
- Pattern: "In 12 months, every AE will be their own GTM engineer."
- Why it works: bold calls get shared and debated.
- Must be specific. "AI will change sales" is too vague.

## Not a moment (skip these)
- Agreement filler: "Totally, great point."
- Setup with no payoff
- Product walkthroughs ("click here, then here")
- Housekeeping, intros, polls, signup pitches
