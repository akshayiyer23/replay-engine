# anti-patterns.md — Automatic Deductions

Each pattern found in a clip subtracts 15 points from the base score.
A clip can trigger multiple penalties.

## 1. Product pitch / sales content
The clip is primarily promoting a product, feature, or company rather than sharing
a genuine insight. Signs: "sign up", "book a demo", "try [product]", listing features
without any broader lesson, announcements that only matter to current users.

## 2. Requires earlier context
The clip cannot stand alone — it references a "this" or "that" or "as I said" without
establishing what it is. A cold viewer would be confused in the first 10 seconds.
Signs: starts mid-thought, relies on a shared assumption or prior slide, pronoun
without antecedent.

## 3. Slow start (hook takes >10 seconds)
The clip spends the opening getting to the point rather than landing a hook immediately.
Estimate ~2.5 words/second: >25 words in the first sentence before any tension/claim
counts as slow. Signs: long scene-setting preamble, "so what I want to talk about today
is…", throat-clearing qualifiers before the actual point.

## 4. Saturated cold-email angle
The clip's core insight is about cold-email tactics (personalisation lines,
subject-line tricks, follow-up sequences) with no fresh angle — the kind of content
that floods GTM feeds. Only penalise if cold-email IS the main topic AND the insight
is generic. Cold-email content with a truly novel data point or contrarian take is exempt.
